FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

We have compiled a list of commonly asked questions regarding treatments, doctors, appointments, and facilities at Parth Super Speciality Hospital, Ranchi.

Parth Hospital is a multi-disciplinary super-speciality centre offering Cardiology & 2D Echo Doppler, Neurosurgery & Spine, Orthopedics & Joint Replacement, Laparoscopic Surgery, Internal Medicine & Chest, Pediatrics & NICU, and 24x7 Critical Care Trauma ICU.

Booking an appointment is seamless. Click the Book Appointment button on our website, select your preferred department and date, or call our direct helpline at +91 6202747361.

Our Emergency, Trauma, ICU, Pathology, and Pharmacy units operate 24 Hours a day, 7 days a week. Routine specialist OPD consultations run Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Yes, Parth Hospital operates a dedicated fleet of Advanced Life Support (ALS) and Basic Life Support (BLS) ambulances equipped with oxygen, cardiac defibrillators, and trained paramedics for rapid pickup across Ranchi and highways.

Yes, we accept major government and private health insurance policies, corporate tie-ups, and Third-Party Administrators (TPAs) for cashless inpatient hospitalization and surgical admissions.

Please bring any previous medical prescriptions, lab reports, X-Rays, 2D Echo or MRI records, list of current medications, and a valid photo identification card (such as Aadhaar).

Yes, our hospital features a specialized Level-3 Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) with modern incubators, warmers, neonatal ventilators, and continuous physiological monitoring managed by experienced pediatricians.

We are conveniently situated on Ring Road, Opposite Jeetu Fuels HP, Chuttu Neori, Ranchi-835217, offering smooth vehicular accessibility and ample parking space.
